Before you participate

When does the ticket sale close?

Ticket requests close at the end of Vijayadashami, 21 October 2026 (India time). The countdown above uses midnight at the start of 22 October as the closing time.

When is my lucky ticket issued?

After you submit your UPI transaction reference, the organizer will verify the payment against the account. The server issues the ticket only after the organizer approves the order. A QR code or submitted reference is not itself proof of a successful payment.

How will the winners be selected?

The organizer should publish the draw method, eligibility rules, result announcement channel and prize-claim process before ticket sales. Please confirm these details with the organizer before paying.
